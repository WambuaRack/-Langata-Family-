Endoscopy Centre

See what's causing the problem

Endoscopy uses a thin, flexible tube with a tiny camera to look inside the body, often to find the cause of stomach or bowel symptoms. Our centre is part of the clinic, so your tests and follow-up care stay connected.

Ask us which endoscopy procedures we offer and how to prepare. We will explain everything before your visit.

  • Clear explanationYou will know what to expect before and after.
  • Joined-up careConsultation, laboratory, ultrasound and pharmacy in one place.
  • Results that matterHistopathology services help confirm what tests find.

Questions patients ask

Do I need an appointment?

Call 0728 835 335 to book or to ask whether you can walk in.

What is histopathology?

It is the laboratory study of tissue samples, such as biopsies, to help diagnose a condition.

How should I prepare for endoscopy?

Preparation depends on the procedure. We will give you clear instructions when you book.

Do you have a pharmacy?

Yes. You can get your prescribed medicines at the clinic.
